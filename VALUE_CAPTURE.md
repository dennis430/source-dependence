# VALUE_CAPTURE.md

What can legitimately accrue to the maintainer (Dennis Liu):

- Maintainer attribution and voluntary citations.
- Actual third-party integrations or reuses.
- Qualified inbound interest arising from demonstrated usefulness.
- A recognizable historical collection of authentic observations (companion archive).

What does not accrue:

- Exclusive rights over the methodology or field.
- Ownership of a "standard."
- Automatic revenue from open-source publication.
- Patent, trademark, or legal-admissibility claims.

Open-source adoption is not automatically revenue. No paid product, consulting funnel, or proprietary lock-in is built before external demand exists.

Contact: via GitHub issues on this repository.
